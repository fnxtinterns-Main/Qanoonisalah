# Qanooni Salah - Modern Legal Technology Platform

India's premier digital legal facilitation platform connecting citizens, startups, and enterprises with Bar Council verified advocates across 100+ practice areas in under 5 minutes.

Designed & Engineered by **[Technxtai](https://technxtai.com)**.

---

## 🎨 Design System & Palette
Adheres strictly to the 60-30-10 legal-tech prestige palette:
* **60% Golden (Dominant):** `#FAF6ED` canvas, `#C5A028` gold gradients, `#D4AF37` metallic borders, and amber accents.
* **30% Black (Structure):** Deep obsidian/charcoal `#0A0A0A` for navigation, footer, typography, and contrast banners.
* **10% Emerald (Highlights):** Vibrant `#059669` for primary high-converting CTAs, verified trust badges, live status indicators, and WhatsApp floating help.

---

## 🚀 Features
* **Bar Council of India Disclaimer:** Mandatory first-visit consent modal compliant with non-solicitation rules.
* **Clean URLs:** Extensionless routing without `.php` or `index.php` (handled by `router.php` and `.htaccess`).
* **Instant Booking System:** Multi-modal intake form (audio call, video strategy, written legal opinion).
* **Practice Areas:** 100+ categories including Property Due Diligence, Startup Incorporation, Documentation, Matrimonial, Criminal Defense & Bail, and Traffic Challan.
* **Advocate Partner Portal:** Dedicated registration & verification channel for practicing attorneys.

---

## 💻 Tech Stack
* **Backend:** PHP 8.x (Modular Component Architecture)
* **Frontend:** Tailwind CSS, Custom Glassmorphism, CSS Grid/Flexbox
* **Icons:** Inline SVGs & Lucide Icons
* **Web Server Support:** Apache (`.htaccess`), Nginx, and PHP Built-in Server (`router.php`)

---

## 🛠️ Local Development

### Run with PHP Built-in Server:
```bash
php -S localhost:8080 router.php
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.
